## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for emergency life-saving workflows where cognitive load must approach zero. The brand personality balances unconditional clinical authority with compassionate, tactile reassurance. In acute crises—such as cardiac events, polytrauma, or severe respiratory distress—callers experience extreme tunnel vision, hand tremors, and sensory overload. The interface rejects aggressive, panic-inducing flashing red sirens in favor of an authoritative, glare-free aesthetic that anchors the citizen while providing clear, instantaneous lifelines.

The visual style blends **Tactile Modernism** with **Calm Institutional Utility**:
- **Tactile Affordances:** Primary emergency triggers employ deliberate, physical interaction metaphors (such as 1000ms hold rings with multi-stage haptic feedback and distinct pressed depth) to prevent fatal misfires while guaranteeing instant, confident activation under physical stress.
- **Warm Reassurance:** By pairing warm mineral cream foundations with deep nocturnal navy, the interface minimizes glare in low-light night conditions and direct noon sunlight alike, establishing psychological trust without sterile coldness.
- **Bilingual Dignity:** Native Devanagari typography receives equal visual weight and optical balance alongside Latin letterforms, honoring regional public infrastructure standards without sacrificing elegance or speed.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around functional psychological hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#14213D` - Deep Navy):** Represents state-level authority, institutional reliability, and structured permanence. Used for dominant typography, structural navigation elements, primary card borders, and critical icon stems.
- **Secondary (`#FF6B5B` - Coral):** The high-urgency operational trigger. Coral replaces harsh crimson with an assertive, warm, high-contrast hue that immediately draws the eye during acute distress without triggering cognitive disorientation. It governs the 1-second hold SOS trigger, live beacon broadcast rings, and critical triage status tags.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Mint/Emerald):** Reassurance and verification. Reserved exclusively for confirmed states—sub-meter GPS locks, active telemetry streaming, assigned ambulances, and hospital readiness handshakes.
- **Neutral (`#F4EDE0` - Cream):** A soft, warm mineral foundation that replaces clinical white. It eliminates blue-light glare during nocturnal emergencies and prevents wash-out in bright outdoor sunlight.
- **Supporting Layers:** `#FFFFFF` provides stark card elevation over the cream background; `#E2D7C5` serves as low-contrast architectural divider lines; `#1F2937` ensures WCAG AAA text contrast across all secondary descriptions and system telemetry labels.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes micro-legibility at arm’s length during trembling hand movement.

- **Headline Stack:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers humanist openness, generous aperture clearances, and distinct counters that prevent letter blurring under low brightness or high-vibration ambulance transit.
- **Body & Numerical Stack:** **Inter** handles high-density telemetry, conversational voice transcripts, instructions, and critical contact numbers with robust tabular lining numbers and high x-height.
- **Bilingual Devanagari Integration:** The system seamlessly falls back to **Noto Sans Devanagari** (`निवारण`) with matched baseline height, standardizing stroke contrast to ensure zero layout jump when switching dynamically between English and Hindi (`hi-IN`).
- **Data Callouts:** ETA tickers and telemetry coordinates use bold proportional numeric figures (`mono-metric`) with tight tracking for immediate legibility at a single glance.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **mobile-first fluid columnar layout** engineered around human thumb-reach ergonomics:

- **Mobile Viewport (360px - 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with a `1rem` margin and `1rem` column gutters. Critical interactive triggers (SOS button, Voice Hotline trigger, and Call 112 fallback) are pinned within the bottom 60% of the screen ("The Thumb Safe Arc").
- **Tablet / In-Vehicle Displays (600px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. The tracking map and telemetry stepper split into a balanced 5:3 two-column configuration.
- **Desktop / CAD Workstation (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at an inner container width of 1440px with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` margins.
- **Emergency Tap Clearances:** Interactive targets maintain an absolute minimum touch zone of 48×48px. The primary SOS container exceeds this at 230×230px, ensuring guaranteed tap target acquisition regardless of motor impairment or ambient vehicle vibration.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through **tactile surface stratification and ambient tinted diffusion**, rather than harsh black drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base mineral cream (`#F4EDE0`). No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Surfaces):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px perimeter border of `#E2D7C5` and an ambient warm shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Sheets & Triage Focus Units):** Elevated White (`#FCF9F3`) with `0 10px 28px -4px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08)` and crisp border articulation.
- **Level 3 (SOS Floating Interactive Core):** Multi-layered tactile depth. The rest state utilizes a double ring shadow: `0 12px 36px rgba(255, 107, 91, 0.35), 0 2px 6px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.12)`. When pressed during the 1-second hold, the elevation translates down 3px with the outer glow compressing to `0 4px 14px rgba(255, 107, 91, 0.50)` for instant physical feedback.
- **Level 4 (Critical Modal / Emergency Pre-Arrival Alert):** Deep Navy banner surfaces (`#14213D`) float above content using `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(14, 24, 45, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The system uses a **Rounded (Level 2)** shape standard with specialized functional extensions:

- **Base Components:** Input fields, status banners, telemetry cards, and sheet panels utilize a default corner radius of `0.75rem` (`rounded-md`) to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Interactive Pills & Status Badges:** Severity selectors (`Critical`, `High`, `Medium`, `Low`), GPS accuracy indicators, and voice extraction tags use full continuous pill rounding (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical selectors from content containers.
- **Tactile SOS Trigger:** A pure geometric circle (`border-radius: 50%`) with an integrated SVG progress track that scales proportionally.
- **Dividers & Stepper Connectors:** Solid 2px pill-ended strokes in `#E2D7C5` for completed steps and `#10B981` for active live milestones.

## Components

### 1. Primary SOS Trigger (1-Second Hold-to-Send)
- **Geometry:** 230px circular tactile button centered within the lower viewport.
- **Colorway:** Emergency Coral background (`#FF6B5B`), Pure White icon and typography (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Interaction Arc:** Includes a concentric SVG stroke (`4px` track width) in Deep Navy at 15% opacity, which fills clockwise with `#14213D` over 1000ms upon sustained user hold.
- **Haptic & Visual States:** Scale transition `scale(0.97)` on press. If released before 1000ms, the ring resets with a gentle elastic ease. A prominent label below reads: *"Hold for 1 sec to trigger"*.

### 2. Severity Selector Pills
- **Geometry:** Pill-shaped capsules (`9999px` radius), minimum 44px tap height, 16px horizontal padding.
- **Default State:** Transparent fill, 1.5px border in `#CFC0A8`, `#14213D` label.
- **Critical Selected State:** Solid Deep Navy (`#14213D`) fill with Coral dot indicator (`#FF6B5B`), text in Pure White.
- **Active Category States:** Distinct tonal tags: Critical (Deep Coral Tint `#FFEAE7`), High (Warm Amber Tint `#FEF3C7`), Moderate (Mint Tint `#E6F8F1`).

### 3. Cards & Telemetry Containers
- **Visuals:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, 16px corner radius, 1px subtle border (`#E2D7C5`), 16px to 20px internal padding.
- **Header:** Label sm uppercase with a status dot indicator (e.g., Mint pulse for GPS lock).
- **Metric Group:** High-contrast pairing of `mono-metric` figure (e.g., `4 min`) alongside secondary descriptor in `#1F2937`.

### 4. Bilingual AI Voice Hotline Interface
- **Ambient Voice Bubble:** Pure White container featuring real-time speech transcription in `#14213D`.
- **"Understood so far" Extraction Badges:** Mini-pills (`rounded-full`) in `#FCF9F3` with a 1px `#E2D7C5` border. Highlighted clinical keywords (e.g., `[Chest Pain]`, `[Location Locked]`) display with subtle Navy bolding.
- **Language Switcher:** Segmented control toggling between `English` and `हिन्दी` with smooth sliding pill highlight.

### 5. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Container:** Minimum 52px height, 12px corner radius, Pure White background, 1.5px border (`#E2D7C5`).
- **Focus State:** 2px solid border in `#14213D` with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08)` focus ring.
- **Labels & Hints:** `#1F2937` font-weight 600 floating label; placeholder text in `#64748B`.

### 6. Four-Step Dispatch Stepper
- **Layout:** Vertical progress tracker with 24px circular indicator nodes connected by a 2px timeline rule.
- **Completed Node:** Solid `#10B981` (Mint/Emerald) with white checkmark icon.
- **Active Node (e.g., "On the way"):** Solid Deep Navy (`#14213D`) with a radiating concentric ring animation in Coral (`#FF6B5B`).
- **Pending Node:** Soft cream fill with a 2px `#E2D7C5` outline and muted secondary typography.

### 7. Action Buttons & Fallback Links
- **Secondary Urgent ("Speak to the hotline"):** Pure White surface, `#14213D` border (2px), 16px border-radius, bold label with leading audio-wave icon.
- **Direct 112 Fallback ("Call 112"):** Ghost button with high-contrast underline or bold border in `#14213D`, providing an immediate telephonic escape hatch that bypasses data connectivity.

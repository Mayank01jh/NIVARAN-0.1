Dispatch Protocols & System Governance

Detailed answers regarding algorithmic decision-making, automated failovers, and dispatcher human authority.

NIVARAN uses a multi-variable engine that analyzes real-time road distances, current traffic choke points, and specific crew credentials (e.g., Critical Care Paramedic vs. EMT-Basic) alongside onboard equipment (ventilators, ECMO, pediatric kits). The CAD generates the most optimal unit recommendation, leaving the dispatcher with full authority to confirm or override with a single tap.
The platform interfaces directly with municipal Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS). When a unit is assigned to a P1 critical code, NIVARAN broadcasts a forward-looking trajectory vector. Traffic lights along the ambulance’s upcoming path cycle through green intervals 45 seconds prior to arrival, reducing intersection collision risks.
In-cab terminals and CAD consoles cache mission critical vectors locally in indexed offline databases. If cellular coverage drops, terminals buffer telemetry and continue providing offline GPS route guidance. Audio bridges instantly fallback to secondary VHF/APCO P25 radio channels with automated telemetry reconstruction once connectivity resumes.

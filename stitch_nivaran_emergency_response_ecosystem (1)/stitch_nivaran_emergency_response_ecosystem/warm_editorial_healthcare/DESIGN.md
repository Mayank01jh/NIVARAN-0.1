## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a welcoming, dignified, and editorial healthcare environment for citizens, patients, and clinical vision partners. Rejecting clinical sterility and cold institutionalism, the visual identity draws inspiration from prestigious literary publications and architectural heritage to deliver an atmosphere of calm authority, empathy, and clarity.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Classicism**:
- **Atmosphere**: Hospitable, authoritative, serene, and deeply trustworthy.
- **Voice**: Measured, clear, and reassuring, prioritizing patient comprehension and dignity.
- **Visual Weight**: Deliberate negative space, disciplined proportions, fine hairline dividing accents, and humanistic typographic cadence.

## Colors

The palette operates under a strict **60:30:10** architectural distribution to maintain editorial serenity and structural visual hierarchy.

- **60% Dominant Neutral (`#F4EDE0` Cream)**: Forms the physical canvas. Used across primary backdrops, canvas surfaces, structural cards, and spacious margins. It eliminates the eye fatigue of stark white while providing warmth.
- **30% Structural Primary (`#14213D` Deep Navy)**: Used for primary typography, authoritative frames, header bars, persistent navigation anchors, and deep structural surfaces. It conveys medical precision, institutional stability, and unyielding reliability.
- **10% Kinetic Accent (`#FF6B5B` Warm Coral)**: Reserved strictly for calls-to-action, critical active states, vital notification indicators, key interactive triggers, and high-priority vision metrics. Coral must never be applied decoratively over large surface areas.

### Palette Ratios & Contrast
- High contrast pairing is maintained between Deep Navy text (`#14213D`) against the warm Cream canvas (`#F4EDE0`), comfortably exceeding WCAG AAA standards.
- Secondary neutral support includes an off-white inner card tint (`#FCF9F5`) and muted navy hairline borders (`rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.12)`).

## Typography

The typographical architecture mirrors traditional medical journals reinterpreted for high-legibility digital screens:

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display)**: Infuses editorial gravitas, patient empathy, and humanity. Characterized by transitional serifs and generous proportions. Used for broad storytelling titles, section openings, and primary portal banners.
- **Interface & Content (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers geometric balance, generous counters, and functional legibility across diagnostic readouts, consultation records, forms, and clinical data tables.
- **Italic Usage**: Playfair Display italic is strictly applied to contextual medical quotes, patient guidance subtitles, and empathetic conversational prompts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy implements a **Restrained Editorial Grid** configured to maximize breathing room and cognitive comfort.

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: Max container width of 1360px centered on screen. Gutters set to `2.5rem` (`gutter-lg`) with outer canvas margins expanding dynamically from `3rem` to `5rem`. Visual sections maintain breathing room with padding blocks scaling in multiples of `2.5rem`.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: Gutters set to `1.5rem` (`gutter`) with canvas margins set to `2rem`. Asymmetrical card arrangements flatten into balanced two-column rows.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: Gutters set to `1rem` (`gutter-sm`) with canvas margins set to `1.25rem` (`margin-sm`). All content reflows linearly to emphasize continuous vertical reading cadence.

Whitespace serves a functional diagnostic purpose: reducing visual stress for vision-impaired patients while maintaining an unhurried, reassuring presence.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is articulated through **Tonal Stratification and Low-Contrast Hairlines** rather than aggressive dropshadows.

- **Canvas Tier**: Primary background canvas set to Cream (`#F4EDE0`).
- **Surface Tier**: Interactive cards and vision modules rest on Warm Alabaster (`#FCF9F5`) enveloped by an ultra-delicate outline of `1px solid rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08)`.
- **Raised Interactive Modules**: Active modal windows and dropdown menus utilize an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.06)`, producing an organic paper lift effect.
- **Deep Immersion Modules**: Occasional inverted clinical panels utilize the deep structural Navy (`#14213D`) as a ground plane with crisp Cream (`#F4EDE0`) typography to delineate clinical diagnostics from general citizen navigation.

## Shapes

The system adopts a **Soft, Tailored Shape Language (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and micro-cards employ an architectural `0.25rem` (4px) curvature, preserving crisp boundaries reminiscent of quality bookbinding and paper sheets.
- Structural viewports, visual symptom cards, and floating hero panels utilize `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status indicator pills and interactive vision tags alone adopt full capsule radiuses to distinctly separate informational metadata from structural content containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (10% Coral)**: Solid `#FF6B5B` fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, padded at `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover transition lightening background to `#FF7F71` with a soft paper shift (`transform: translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (30% Navy)**: Solid `#14213D` fill with `#F4EDE0` text. Applied to confirmation dialogues, medical portal access, and dossier exports.
- **Editorial Ghost Action**: Transparent background with a `1px solid rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.2)` border, `#14213D` text, transitioning on hover to solid Cream tint (`rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.04)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FCF9F5`. Border: `1px solid rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.16)`. Radius: `0.25rem`.
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans Body-MD in Deep Navy (`#14213D`).
- Focus State: Border transitions seamlessly to Coral (`#FF6B5B`) with an outer subtle halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(255, 107, 91, 0.15)`).

### Cards & Vision Portals
- Cream/Alabaster paper surface (`#FCF9F5`) banded with a discrete hairline border (`rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.08)`).
- Cards balance asymmetric padding: `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) internally for desktops to emphasize calm clarity. Header inside cards pairs a Playfair Display title with a minimalist monoline icon (1.5px stroke weight).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Radio controls and checkboxes feature a 1.5px border in `#14213D`. Checked state fills directly with Coral (`#FF6B5B`) containing a crisp white tick or inner pip.

### Chips & Clinical Badges
- Surface: Cream base with Navy borders (`rgba(20, 33, 61, 0.12)`). Typography: Label-SM uppercase with `0.06em` tracking.
- Active triage and urgency badges utilize a soft Coral wash (`rgba(255, 107, 91, 0.12)`) with solid `#FF6B5B` text.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Acuity & Vision Scale Strip**: A calibrated horizontal interactive diagnostic component using sharp contrast tick marks in Deep Navy with Coral reticle indicators.
- **Citizen Diagnostic Summary Ledger**: An editorial table utilizing alternating Cream bands, Playfair Display row titles, and Plus Jakarta Sans tabular numeric readouts.
